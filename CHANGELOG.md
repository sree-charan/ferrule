## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Summary
First release of Ferrule: a menu bar toolbox for macOS that runs entirely on the Mac, with no account, no telemetry and no server of its own.

### Added
- Ferrule's own name, icon and graphite and safety-orange look, including a nut mark in the menu bar.

### Removed
- The automatic update check and the in-app updater. Ferrule never contacts a release server; a new version is a new build.
- Temporary links for screenshots and recordings, their upload shortcut and their settings. Sharing goes through the system Share menu.
- The feedback form.
- The downloaded AI price list. Costs use the list shipped with the app.
- Donation, community and social links, and the support page.
